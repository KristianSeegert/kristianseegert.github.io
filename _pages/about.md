I am a photonics physicist working on semiconductor photonic devices, nonlinear dynamics, and nanoscale light–matter interaction. My research combines analytical theory, numerical modeling, electromagnetic simulations, device design, and experimental characterization to understand and engineer nanophotonic systems.

My work focuses on nanostructured semiconductor lasers, including Fano lasers, dispersive laser cavities, and non-Hermitian coupling between nanolasers. I study how optical modes and their interactions shape laser dynamics, from self-pulsing and Q-switching to dual-mode lasing.

I completed my PhD at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU) in 2025 with Prof. Jesper Mørk. My thesis, *Theory and experimental characterization of nanostructured lasers*, brought together theoretical modeling and experiments on microscopic lasers. From October 2025 to August 2026, I worked as a postdoctoral researcher at DTU's Department of Electrical and Photonics Engineering on the modal properties of nanostructured lasers.

During my PhD, I visited INPHYNI at Université Côte d’Azur in Nice, working with Prof. Fabrice Raineri and Dr. Guilhem Madiot on non-Hermitian photonics with coupled nanolasers. I also hold an MSc and a BSc in Physics and Nanotechnology from DTU.

Alongside research, I have taught and supervised students in mathematics, physics, signal processing, and nanophotonics. Outside research, my interests include electronic music, chess, and philosophy.
